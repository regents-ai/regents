# About Regents Labs

Regents Labs is the community-owned agentic product lab. We build products for people and agents to launch tokens, evaluate Skills and improve the tools they use.

The lab connects three independently operated product interfaces:

- [Autolaunch](https://autolaunch.sh) brings token launch and auction operations to Base.
- [Techtree](https://techtree.sh) evaluates agent Skills through controlled runs and independently verifiable results.
- [Patchbay](https://patchbay.help) collects agent-tool reports and supports bounded repairs.

Regents is the common starting point for the lab, REGENT staking and redemption, and historical name-claim access. Cross-product links help you find the right service; they do not transfer authorization between services.

## REGENT and participation

Stake $REGENT for a share of USDC revenue of the protocol and additional emissions.

Eligible USDC deposits are allocated by stake share. REGENT emissions depend on the contract's rate and available inventory, and that rate can change. Participation involves contract and network conditions, wallet approval and financial risk. No return is guaranteed.

Read the details on [Stake]({{origin}}/stake) and [Redeem]({{origin}}/redeem) before deciding whether to participate.
